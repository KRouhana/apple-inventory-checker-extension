A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A helpful signal.
No stock guarantees.

Availability changes quickly. The extension gives you a recent result; Apple makes the final confirmation.

Does Chrome need to stay open?

Yes. Checks run locally while Chrome is running and your computer is awake and connected to the internet. Closing Chrome or putting your computer to sleep stops or delays checks. The popup itself does not need to stay open.

What does “Unknown” mean?

It means the extension cannot verify availability yet. A blocked request, connection problem, or missing product or store result is not proof that an item is out of stock. Check the watch’s explanation in settings for more detail.

Does it reserve or buy a phone for me?

No. Inventory Signal does not reserve stock, add items to a cart, sign in to Apple, or place an order. “Open at Apple” takes you to the selected product. Confirm your configuration, store, and pickup availability with Apple before purchasing.

How do notifications work?

Enable desktop notifications in settings and allow them in Chrome and your operating system. Telegram is optional: connect your own bot through private pairing, tap Start in Telegram, then send a test notification. Checks and alerts can be delayed, and stock may change before you act.

Where is my data stored?

Watches, check history, and optional Telegram connection credentials are stored in your browser. Requests go directly to Apple and, if you use Telegram, to Telegram. There is no publisher analytics or monitoring server. Read the privacy details.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Inventory Signal supports desktop Google Chrome and the iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max, and Duo catalog for the United States, Canada, and United Kingdom. A catalog entry does not guarantee that Apple is currently offering pickup for it.
