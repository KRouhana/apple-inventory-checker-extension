YOUR BROWSER. YOUR WATCHES.

Privacy, plainly.

Last updated September 18, 2026

Inventory Signal checks Apple pickup availability from your browser. The extension has no publisher-operated monitoring server, account system, or telemetry.

At a glance: your watch data stays in local browser storage. Availability requests go to Apple. If you enable personal Telegram notifications, authentication and notification requests go directly to Telegram.

What the extension stores

The extension uses local browser storage for your selected products and stores, watches, settings, check history and results, and optional personal Telegram connection details. These Telegram details include the bot credential and chat information needed to deliver your notifications. Local storage is associated with your Chrome profile.

Public Apple store metadata may be cached locally so the extension can show store names and locations. A postal code entered for store lookup is sent transiently to Apple; it is not retained as part of the watch configuration.

Saved Telegram connection details are encrypted with AES-GCM. A non-extractable WebCrypto key is stored separately in the extension’s IndexedDB. This protects the stored representation; it is not an operating-system credential vault and does not protect against malicious extension code or a compromised browser profile. Watch settings and public stock history are ordinary local browser data.

Requests to Apple

The extension sends your selected product, country, store, and lookup parameters to Apple’s services to discover stores and check pickup availability. Apple receives those requests, including your IP address, under its own privacy practices.

Browser-managed Apple cookies may accompany fixed requests to Apple. The extension does not read or extract those cookies. It does not ask for your Apple password, payment details, or order information. When you open a product at Apple, any browsing or purchase activity on Apple’s website is handled by Apple.

Optional personal Telegram notifications

Connecting a bot is optional. If you choose to connect one, the extension sends the bot credential directly to api.telegram.org to authenticate requests. It checks the private pairing interaction to identify the chat you authorized and sends test or availability messages to that chat.

Your bot credential and chat details are used only for requests to Telegram’s API, not sent to the publisher. Notification messages include relevant watched product, store, and availability information. Telegram receives and processes these requests and messages under its own privacy practices.

A pairing link can expire before setup is finished. The saved connected-bot configuration does not have that setup-link expiry. Disconnecting the integration removes the extension’s saved connection; revoking a bot token or removing messages already delivered in Telegram must be done through Telegram.

No advertising or publisher analytics

The extension does not include advertising, publisher analytics, or telemetry, and the publisher does not receive your watches or check history. This website does not load third-party fonts, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels.

Limited Use

Inventory Signal uses information only to provide its stated monitoring and notification features. Its handling of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising.

This website and GitHub

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operation of its hosting service. Downloads, source code, and issue reports are also hosted on GitHub and are subject to GitHub’s own privacy practices.

Public issue reports can be read by others. Do not include bot tokens, chat IDs, postal codes, or other personal information in reports or screenshots.

Your controls

  • Pause watches to stop their scheduled checks. Closing Chrome or sleeping your computer also stops or delays local checks.
  • Manage watches and notification settings in the extension’s settings page.
  • Disconnect Telegram if you no longer want the extension to use that integration.
  • Remove the extension from Chrome to delete its local extension storage. This does not remove Telegram messages, data held by Apple or GitHub, or files you downloaded separately.

Questions and updates

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the project repository without sharing personal data. This policy will be updated if the extension’s data handling changes.

Inventory Signal is independent software and is not affiliated with Apple, Google, or Telegram.
